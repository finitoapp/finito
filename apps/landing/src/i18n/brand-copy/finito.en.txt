landing.tech.account.kyc.body
Finito never holds or forwards your money. A bank transfer goes from the customer's bank straight to your account, which your bank has already verified. Bitcoin lands in a wallet only you hold the keys to. So the app has no money of yours that would require verifying you.

landing.tech.account.why.body
Sign-up is mostly for the provider's benefit. They need to know whose data sits on their server and let only you in. Finito keeps none of your data on a server, so there's no account to create. What lets you in isn't our server, it's your key.

landing.tech.backup.diagram.caption
With a cloud app, the provider holds the key to your data. So they can read it, and so can anyone who gets to the server: support, engineers, sales, or an attacker in a breach. With Finito, the relay sees only encrypted noise, and only you have the key. Not even our support can see your data — they only know what you tell them.

landing.tech.backup.where.body
The backup goes to Finito's relay (live-relay.finito.fit) and, at the same time, to the relay of Linky (evolu.linky.fit), a friendly project built on the same principles. If one goes down, the other keeps going. In the settings you can turn them off or add your own, for example on your own server.

landing.tech.money.card.body
On a device with a Switchio terminal, Finito only hands the amount to the Switchio app. Switchio and the merchant's bank then process the card, just as with any other terminal. Card details never reach Finito.

landing.tech.money.lightning.body
Lightning is a network for instant bitcoin payments. Finito issues an invoice from your Spark wallet, which runs right inside the app. The payment lands in your wallet, and only your device holds its keys. Transactions are processed by Spark network operators and a Lightning gateway provider — they see the amount and the time. The app gets the exchange rate for your currency from the public Yadio service.

landing.tech.money.qr.check

landing.tech.security.ownership.body
Whoever holds your data can analyse it, sell it, hand it over on request or lose it. With Finito, only you can read it, and nobody can make us hand it over, because we don't have it.

landing.tech.security.target.body
A server holding the data of thousands of merchants is an ideal target: one breach and the attacker has everything. And most breaches don't come from cracked encryption, but from a misconfigured database, a stolen admin password or an employee with access to everything. Finito has no such server. An attacker would have to break into each device separately, and on the relays they'd find only encrypted data.

landing.tech.security.tracking.body
Finito uses no analytics and no tracking services. Error reporting stays off until you turn it on yourself. And when you do, recovery phrases and account numbers are stripped from the report before it's sent.

landing.tech.transparency.body
Only the key has to stay secret. Cryptographers call this Kerckhoffs's principle, and Finito is built on it: anyone can read the code, but the key — your recovery phrase — is yours alone.

landing.tech.transparency.build.body
